I am a first-year PhD student at the Center for Doctoral Studies in Social and Behavioral Sciences at the University of Mannheim. My research interests lie at the intersection of online toxicity, hate speech, and right-wing radicalization. I am particularly interested in leveraging natural language processing (NLP) methods and large language models (LLMs) to better understand and reduce toxicity in online spaces. My work also examines how online discourse evolves over time and how certain forms of toxic or extremist behavior become normalized within digital communities.

If you are interested in similar topics or research, please reach out!
